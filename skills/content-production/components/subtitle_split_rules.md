# Subtitle Split Rules

Use this component when writing `projects/<slug>/final/subtitle.md`.

## Rules

- One subtitle line should contain one clear spoken unit.
- Prefer 8-18 Chinese characters per line.
- Avoid more than 24 Chinese characters in one line.
- Split at commas, pauses, transitions, and meaning boundaries.
- Do not split names, numbers, or fixed terms awkwardly.
- Remove filler words unless they help tone.
- Keep a natural spoken rhythm.

## Format

Default output is plain text lines under `## 字幕正文`.

Do not generate `.srt` in V1 unless the user explicitly asks.
