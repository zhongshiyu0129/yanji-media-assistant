# Hook Opening

Goal: rewrite the opening so the first 3 seconds create suspense, scale, curiosity, and momentum while staying fact-grounded.

## Hook types

- Question: "你有没有发现..."
- Contrast: "很多人以为 X，其实真正关键的是 Y。"
- Cognitive reversal: "今天我用一条视频讲清楚，为什么这件事可能颠覆你的认知。"
- Conflict: "这件事最容易被忽略，但它恰恰决定了结果。"
- Strong spoken hook: "你敢相信吗..." or "万万没有想到..."
- Nostalgic empathy: start from shared life memory, era change, or ordinary people's experience.
- Scale shock: make a number visible through comparison.
- Future reveal: frame the topic as something the audience thought they knew but will now understand differently.

## Rules

- Produce 3 candidate openings.
- The hook must match the body.
- Strong language is allowed only when the body supports it.
- Avoid fake conflict, fake certainty, and unrelated clickbait.
- Keep hooks rigorous, rational, and objective; do not use fear, anger, shame, or group opposition as the engine of attention.
- Avoid absolutist hook language unless the claim is directly supported by evidence.
- Do not flatten the hook. Rigor means supported and precise, not bland.
- Prefer vivid suspense over generic seriousness.
- Learn from the original's gold lines, emotional rhythm, scale comparisons, and curiosity gaps, but rewrite them in the user's own voice.
- Finish the opening in 2-5 short sentences.
- Enter the core topic fast.
